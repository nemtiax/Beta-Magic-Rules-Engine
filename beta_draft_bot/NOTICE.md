The optional repository-level `tools/download_card_images.py` utility can
retrieve images from Scryfall for local use. Its shared output is excluded from
the repository and remains subject to the rights and usage terms applicable to
that content.

The factual Beta card metadata snapshot was obtained on September 5, 2026 from
[Scryfall's card search API](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=e%3Aleb&unique=cards&order=name).
Each record links to its specific Scryfall card page. Alternate basic-land art
is collapsed by card name. The printed rules text used by the preview pane was
imported from this project's Limited Edition Beta MTGJSON snapshot. Card images
are excluded.
